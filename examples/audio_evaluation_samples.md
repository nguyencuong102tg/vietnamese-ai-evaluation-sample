# Audio Evaluation Sample Feedback

Use these as **English-written justifications** when reviewing Vietnamese audio clips.

The utterances below are synthetic examples for portfolio demonstration only.

## Sample 1 — Natural Northern Vietnamese
**Utterance:**
```text
Bé nhà tôi bị sốt ba ngày nay.
```

**Feedback:**
```text
The audio sounds natural and native. The tone marks are realized correctly, especially the question-like falling contour on "ba" and the clear final consonant in "nay". The sentence rhythm is appropriate for everyday spoken Vietnamese. I would rate this as Excellent.
```

## Sample 2 — Understandable but tone-mark issue
**Utterance:**
```text
Tôi muốn hỏi anh ấy có đến không.
```

**Feedback:**
```text
The meaning is mostly understandable, but the tonal realization on "hỏi" is flat and may be confused with a level tone in fast speech. The final word "không" is also slightly nasalized and unclear. I would rate this as Acceptable because the task is fulfilled, but the tonal clarity needs improvement.
```

## Sample 3 — Southern-style naturalness
**Utterance:**
```text
Con nhỏ đó nói chuyện dễ thương ghê.
```

**Feedback:**
```text
The recording uses a natural Southern register, with word choices such as "con nhỏ đó" and "dễ thương". The speaker sounds native for the Southern variety. A small weakness is the final "ghê", which is somewhat clipped, but intelligibility remains high. I would rate this as Good.
```

## Sample 4 — Machine-generated intonation
**Utterance:**
```text
Vui lòng xác nhận thông tin trước khi gửi.
```

**Feedback:**
```text
The speech is fluent, but the intonation is overly even and lacks the natural pause before "trước khi gửi". The phrase boundary between "xác nhận thông tin" and "trước khi gửi" is not clearly marked, making the sentence sound mechanically smooth. I would rate this as Good for intelligibility but not fully authentic.
```

## Sample 5 — Register mismatch
**Utterance:**
```text
Ê, mày đưa cái đó qua đây coi.
```

**Feedback:**
```text
The audio sounds native and clear, but the register is very casual and slightly abrupt. It is appropriate for informal speech, but if the prompt expected polite customer-service language, the tone would be inappropriate. I would rate this as Acceptable for linguistic accuracy, with a note on register.
```

## Sample 6 — Medical term mispronunciation
**Utterance:**
```text
Bệnh nhân có dấu hiệu khó thở.
```

**Feedback:**
```text
The sentence is mostly understandable, but "dấu hiệu" is pronounced with an unnatural stress pattern, and "khó thở" sounds rushed. These issues slightly reduce clarity in a medical context, where key symptom terms must be unambiguous. I would rate this as Acceptable and note the term clarity.
```

## Recommended short patterns
For assessment tasks, keep each justification to 2–4 sentences:
1. Overall intelligibility.
2. Linguistic issue: tone, rhythm, register, clarity, or word choice.
3. Rating or recommendation.
