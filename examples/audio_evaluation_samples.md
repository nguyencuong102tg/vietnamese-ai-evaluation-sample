# Audio Evaluation Sample Feedback

Use these as **English-written justifications** when reviewing Vietnamese audio clips.

## Sample 1 — Natural speech
```text
The audio sounds natural and native. The speaker uses smooth intonation, and all words are easy to understand. The sentence stress is appropriate. I would rate this as Excellent.
```

## Sample 2 — Understandable but non-native rhythm
```text
The speaker is understandable, but the intonation sounds slightly flat and the pause between words is unnatural. The pronunciation of vowels is mostly correct, but the rhythm is not native-like. I would rate this as Acceptable.
```

## Sample 3 — Machine-generated or model output
```text
The speech sounds fluent but mechanically smooth. Some phrases lack the natural micro-pauses typical of Vietnamese speech, and the pitch does not vary enough for a real conversational speaker. I would rate this as Good for intelligibility but not fully authentic.
```

## Sample 4 — Unclear final clause
```text
The first part of the sentence is clear, but the final clause is rushed and one word is hard to identify. The overall message is still guessable from context. I would rate this as Acceptable because intelligibility is reduced near the end.
```

## Sample 5 — Incorrect stress/tone
```text
The speaker misplaces stress on an important word, which changes the meaning slightly. The audio is mostly clear, but the tonal quality is not fully natural. I would rate this as Acceptable and note the tone issue.
```
